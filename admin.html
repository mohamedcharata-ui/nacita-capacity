Formula Reference

RC= Available Resources × Utilization × Efficiency
Available= # FTE or Units × Capacity Constraint per Unit
Utilization= (Total Available − Planned Stoppages − Planned Idle) ÷ Total Available
Efficiency= 1 − Unexpected Loss Ratio
